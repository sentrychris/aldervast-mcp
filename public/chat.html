Ask about Aldervast

Ask anything about the game: classes, quests, dungeons, bosses, items, servers. Claude looks the answer up on the Aldervast wiki and links to its sources.

It remembers your last few questions until you leave the page, so you can follow up. An answer takes 10 to 40 seconds.

Answers are written by Claude, an AI, from the wiki. They can be wrong; the linked wiki pages are the source. Questions are sent to Anthropic's API to be answered and are not stored on this site.
